Questadon is a tabletop role-playing game studio run by Sarah Brown in Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what this website (questadon.com) collects about you, why, and what you can do about it. "I", "me", and "Questadon" all mean the same person here.

If anything here is unclear, email me at {% email %}.

## The short version

- I collect your **email address** only if you give it to me, and only use it to send you what you signed up for.
- I use **analytics** to see how many people visit and which pages they use. It doesn't tell me who you are.
- Signup forms use **bot checks** to keep spam out.
- I don't sell your information, and there are no ads on this site.
- You can **unsubscribe** from any email with one click, and ask me to delete your information at any time.

## What I collect, and why

### Your email address

Some pages have forms where you can give me your email address, for example to join my email list, get a free download, or hear when a game is released.

I use your address to send you what you asked for, and occasional Questadon news. If a form will also add you to my general email list, it says so before you sign up. You'll be asked to confirm your address before anything is sent.

I also record *which* form you used, so you get the right email and I know what you were interested in.

Email addresses are stored with the service I use to send my emails (currently [EmailOctopus](https://emailoctopus.com/legal/privacy)). It also records whether you open my emails and click links in them, which helps me see what's useful.

### How people use the site (analytics)

This site uses [Google Analytics](https://policies.google.com/privacy) to count visits and understand how the site is used: which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors are (country or city, not your address), what kind of device and browser you're using, which site sent you here, and actions like downloading a file or following a link.

Google Analytics uses cookies to tell one visit from the next. It doesn't give me your name or email address, and I don't try to identify individual visitors. You can block it with your browser's privacy settings, a content blocker, or [Google's opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### Keeping out spam (bot checks)

To stop bots from signing strangers up to my emails, signup forms use a bot-check service, such as [Cloudflare Turnstile](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstilepolicy/) or [Google reCAPTCHA](https://policies.google.com/privacy). These look at information about your browser and device (and your IP address) to decide whether you're a person. Most of the time you won't see anything.

### Website hosting

The site is hosted by [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/). Like any web host, Netlify processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver pages, and to limit how often a form can be sent from one address.

### Embedded content

Some pages include content from other services, such as YouTube videos or files loaded from public code libraries. Those services see your IP address when your browser loads them, and their own privacy policies apply. Where I can, I use privacy-friendly options (for example, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode).

## Who I share it with

Only the kinds of services described above, and only so they can do their job for this site. I don't sell or rent your information, and I don't share it with advertisers.

I may disclose information if the law requires it.

### Where it's stored

These services may store and process information outside Canada, including in the United States and the European Union. When that happens, your information may be subject to the laws of those countries.

## How long I keep it

- **Email addresses** stay on my list until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete them. After you unsubscribe, my email service keeps a record that you opted out, so you aren't emailed again by mistake.
- **Analytics data** is kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics, after which Google deletes it.

## Your choices and rights

- **Unsubscribe** from my emails any time, using the link at the bottom of every email.
- **Ask what I have** about you, ask me to **correct** it, or ask me to **delete** it, by emailing {% email %}. I'll reply within 30 days.
- **Block cookies and analytics** in your browser settings. The site still works without them.

If you're not happy with how I've handled your information, please tell me first so I can fix it. You can also contact the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/).

## Children

This site isn't aimed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has signed up, email me and I'll delete it.

## Other sites and apps

This policy covers questadon.com only. It doesn't cover:

- apps and tools I make that run separately from this site (such as ones with their own accounts), which have their own policies, or
- other sites I link to, like social media, community spaces, crowdfunding platforms, or stores selling my games.

Each has its own privacy policy.

## Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I'll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change affects how I use your email address, I'll tell you by email first.
